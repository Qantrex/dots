about

homerow is an unofficial project inspired by monkeytype.com. It has no accounts and no server. Your settings and history stay in this browser.

how speed is measured

wpm
Characters in correctly typed words, plus the spaces after them, divided by 5, per minute. In time mode a correct partial last word counts too.
raw
All typed characters per minute divided by 5: correct, incorrect, extra and spaces.
accuracy
Correct keypresses as a share of all keypresses. Fixing a typo with backspace doesn't undo the wrong press.
consistency
How steady your raw speed was from second to second, on a 0-100 scale. Key consistency measures the same over the gaps between keypresses.
burst
Your speed over a single word.
